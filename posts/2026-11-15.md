=== Minnesota forecast
Today :: Clear skies or high clouds. Winds turn southerly and pick up. Gusts around 18 to 21 mph in western Minnesota.
Mon to Wed :: Southerly flow keeps warming temperatures. Wednesday highs reach the mid to upper 50s, about 15 degrees above normal for mid-November. Dry.
Thursday :: A weak system moves out of the Rockies. 20 to 30 percent chance of rain. Forecasters see little moisture and weak lift, and the models show little to no rain amounts. A slight drop in temperature.
Rest of the week :: Dry through late in the week. A couple of systems arrive heading into next weekend (see On the radar).
